In Korean mythology, the **Four Celestial Guardians** (known as **Sasin** or **Sasindo**) are four divine beasts that protect the cardinal directions, seasons, and cosmic balance.

For this lamp I chose two:

**Baekho** (White Tiger): The guardian of the West and Autumn, associated with the element of Metal, strength, and justice.

**Jujak** (Vermilion Bird): The guardian of the South and Summer, associated with the element of Fire, brilliance, and transformation.
